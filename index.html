02 / What I do

Good software starts with understanding the problem.

  • Software development — Thoughtful web applications, websites and internal tools, built around the people who use them.
  • Technical consultancy — An experienced perspective on architecture, technical choices and the practical next step.
  • Systems integration — Connect applications, APIs and data so your systems work together.

03 / How I work

Clear thinking. Direct communication. Accountable delivery.

  • Direct communication — You speak directly with the person doing the technical work. Questions and decisions stay visible.
  • Clear scope — Agree the problem, priorities and boundaries before getting into the build.
  • Realistic commitments — Discuss constraints and trade-offs early, with milestones grounded in the work.
  • Accountable delivery — Review progress together, surface risks and take responsibility for the agreed work.
