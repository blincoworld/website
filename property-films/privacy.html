PROPERTY FILM ENQUIRIES

Your privacy

Piers Blinco uses the details you submit to assess your property for a cinematic promotional film and respond to your enquiry. This includes your contact details, property listing, number of properties and photography information.

How your details are handled

Enquiries are stored in Business OS, hosted using Cloudflare. When enabled, Formspree also processes a copy to deliver enquiry notifications. We also record the submission date and any campaign or referring page information available with your enquiry.

We use this information to take steps at your request before a potential contract and for our legitimate interest in managing enquiries. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Your choices

You can ask Piers for access to, correction of or deletion of your enquiry using the email or WhatsApp conversation through which you received this page. Details are kept only as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting business relationship. You may also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
