How we work

Your agency stays in front. I handle the agreed technical work.

  1. Explore the opportunity — Start with a conversation about the client request, requirements and feasibility. Or talk about the technical services you'd like to offer.
  2. Define the work — Establish scope, dependencies, risks and a delivery estimate. Complex projects may need a separately paid discovery or scoping engagement first.
  3. Agree the commercial approach — I quote confidentially to your agency. You add your margin, control your client pricing and retain the commercial relationship.
  4. Deliver under your brand — I deliver the agreed technical solution behind the scenes, working with your team around the agreed scope and engagement.

Project-specific quotations and flexible engagements. Feasibility, delivery dates and fixed implementation prices depend on proper discovery and an agreed scope.
