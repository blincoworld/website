THE CONVERSATION YOU KNOW

Ever had a client ask for something you weren't sure you could deliver?

The website is going well. Then the client asks about something that sits outside the normal brief.

Illustrative agency-owner scenarios—not testimonials or past-project claims.

“Could it talk to our other systems?”

The CRM, ecommerce platform and internal tools all need connecting. Before you quote, you need to know what those systems can actually do.

“Could we add AI to this?”

The idea sounds promising. The useful question is what problem it should solve, what information it needs and where human judgement stays involved.

“Can you build the whole application?”

HOW THE PARTNERSHIP WORKS

Your agency stays in front.
I handle the agreed technical work.

Each engagement is quoted around the project, its uncertainties and the agreed scope.

Complicated projects may need a separately paid discovery or scoping engagement before a fixed implementation quotation is possible. We'll discuss that before you commit.

  1. Start with a conversation

    Talk through the client request and initial technical discovery.

  2. Define requirements and feasibility

    Find out what needs to happen, what is possible and what still needs checking.

  3. Make the technical work visible

    Scope, dependencies, risks and a delivery estimate—not a guessed deadline.

  4. Get a confidential agency quotation

    A proposal for the agreed work, with project-specific commercial terms.

  5. Add your margin

    You control your pricing and retain the commercial client relationship.

  6. Deliver under your brand

    I deliver the agreed technical solution behind the scenes.
